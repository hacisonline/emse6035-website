Critical path

The chain of activities that cannot slip without moving a deliverable

The project is a strict pipeline: each deliverable is the input to the next, and the final survey has to be in the field over Thanksgiving week before any final analysis can happen. That makes almost every project activity critical. The diagram shows the chain by deliverable; the table underneath is computed from data/activities.csv and shows, for each critical activity, how many days of float sit between its due date and the start of whatever depends on it.

Where the risk concentrates

The two links with no give at all are the pilot-to-final-survey handoff (the pilot analysis is due Nov 1 and the final survey Nov 15, with HW9 and HW10 and Quiz 5 in the same fortnight) and the fielding-to-report link (data arrives during Thanksgiving week and the report is due the Sunday after the final exam). The practical defence is to scaffold clean_data.R, modeling.R and analysis.R on the pilot data in early November so that when the final data lands they only need re-running.
